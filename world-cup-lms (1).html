The Rules
1.Each round, pick one team to qualify for the next round (i.e. win their match — or draw, depending on the draw rule).
2.If your team qualifies, you survive. Group stage: a draw may eliminate you (see draw rule). Knockout rounds: no draws exist — if your team loses (including on pens after extra time), you're eliminated.
3.You can never pick the same team twice across the whole tournament.
ℹKnockout rounds have no draws — extra time & penalties decide. If your team loses at any point (including on pens), you're out.
4.Miss the pick deadline = your pick is void and you're eliminated.
5.Last person standing wins the pot. If multiple players are eliminated in the same round leaving nobody alive, the pot is split equally between those who survived the longest. Same rule applies if multiple players make it to the end.
